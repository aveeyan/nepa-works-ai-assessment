## Aperture Science "The Cake"

### Ingredients
- 2.5 cups Flour (Unbleached, all-purpose)
- 1.5 cups Granulated Sugar (Non-GLaDOS approved)
- 0.75 cups Cocoa Powder (Dark, dutch-processed)
- 2 tsp Baking Soda
- 1 tsp Baking Powder
- 0.5 tsp Salt
- 2 Eggs (Free-range, preferably from test subject café)
- 1 cup Milk (Whole)
- 0.5 cup Vegetable Oil
- 2 tsp Vanilla Extract
- 1 cup Hot Water
- 1 tsp Instant Coffee (Optional, for enhanced flavor)

### Frosting Ingredients
- 1 cup Heavy Cream
- 250g Dark Chocolate (70% cocoa)
- 0.5 cup Unsalted Butter
- 1 cup Powdered Sugar
- 1 tsp Vanilla Extract

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation
1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F)
2. Grease and flour two 9-inch cake pans
3. Ensure pan surfaces are portal-clean (No moon-dust residue)

### Step 2: Dry Mix
1. In a large bowl, sift flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, baking powder, and salt
2. Mix thoroughly (Minimum 3 minutes)
3. In another bowl (Optional: The cake is a lie bowl), combine eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla

### Step 3: Combine
1. Add wet ingredients to dry mixture
2. Mix on medium speed (Or use AI assistance)
3. Stir in hot water slowly (Avoid splashing)
4. Combine until smooth (Do not overmix)

### Step 4: Baking
1. Pour batter evenly into prepared pans
2. Bake for 28-32 minutes (Your test chamber timer)
3. Let cool completely before frosting

### Step 5: Frosting
1. Heat cream until hot (Not boiling)
2. Pour over chopped chocolate
3. Let stand 5 minutes (Patience required)
4. Stir until smooth (No lumps allowed)
5. Add butter, sugar, and vanilla
6. Whip until thick and spreadable

### Step 6: Assembly
1. Place first cake layer on serving plate
2. Add frosting layer (Approximately 1 cm thick)
3. Add second cake layer
4. Frost top and sides completely
5. Decorate with Aperture Science logo (Optional)
