# Aperture Science Weighted Storage Cube: Handling and Usage Guide

## Product Overview
The Weighted Storage Cube is a fundamental testing element in Aperture Science Enrichment Center activities. Its mass distribution properties make it invaluable for puzzle-solving and obstruction management.

## Technical Specifications
- **Weight**: Approximately 22 kg
- **Dimensions**: 1m x 1m x 1m (standard cube)
- **Material**: Carbon-fiber composite with weight-distribution core
- **Color**: Standard white (with blue Aperture Science logo)
- **Durability**: Withstands 5000+ portal uses

## Usage Instructions

### Basic Operation
1. **Lifting**: Use both hands on designated grip points
2. **Carrying**: Keep cube level and close to body
3. **Placing**: Gently set down on pressure plates
4. **Throwing**: Only when necessary for gap crossing

### Portal Interactions
- Can be carried through portals
- Maintains momentum when dropped through portals
- Creates sound-based pressure plate activation
- Useful for blocking energy pellets

## Advanced Applications

### Turret Deflection
- Position cube between self and turrets
- Cube absorbs bullets for approximately 4 seconds
- Use distraction window to activate turret disable switch

### High-Energy Pellet Redirection
- Reflect pellets at precise angles
- Unlock secret testing chambers
- WARNING: Do not attempt without proper training

### Companion Cube Alternative
- When companion cube unavailable
- Provides minimal emotional support
- Does not include heart-shaped decal

## Safety Precautions
- DO NOT drop on feet (Risk of severe injury)
- Use caution near ledge edges
- Report cracked or damaged cubes immediately
- Do not attempt to paint cubes (This invalidates portal adhesion)

## Maintenance
- Weekly: Weight calibration check
- Monthly: Surface integrity inspection
- Annual: Full core replacement (if needed)
