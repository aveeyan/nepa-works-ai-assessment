# Aperture Science Emergency Evacuation Protocol

## Overview
This document outlines emergency procedures for all Enrichment Center personnel and test subjects. Memorize these protocols for your safety.

## Emergency Types

### Class 1: Fire
- **Response**: Immediate evacuation
- **Alarm**: Continuous siren (High pitch)
- **Assembly Point**: Main reception area

### Class 2: Neurotoxin Leak
- **Response**: Seal all testing chambers
- **Alarm**: Warning beep pattern (3-beep sequence)
- **Assembly Point**: Roof level emergency platform
- **Duration**: Remains active until GLaDOS declares safe

### Class 3: Portal Instability
- **Response**: Cease all portal usage
- **Alarm**: Distinctive "Portal malfunction" voice announcement
- **Assembly Point**: Portal-free zone (Specified by AI)

### Class 4: GLaDOS Malfunction
- **Response**: Proceed to emergency testing chamber
- **Alarm**: GLaDOS voice: "This is a test"
- **Assembly Point**: Designated by Morality Core

## Evacuation Steps

### Step 1: Alert
- Announce emergency type over PA system
- Activate proper evacuation alarm
- Alert GLaDOS of situation (If functional)

### Step 2: Assembly
- Move to assigned assembly point
- Wait for role call
- Do not use portals (Unstable during emergencies)

### Step 3: Roll Call
- Test subjects counted by AI
- Personnel check-in with supervisor
- Missing personnel reported immediately

### Step 4: Evacuation Transportation
- Emergency rail system (Manual override available)
- Aerial transport (For roof-level assembly)
- Underground tunnel network (Only for authorized personnel)

## Required Emergency Equipment

### Personnel Kit
1. Emergency portal device (Standard or blue/orange)
2. Light-bearing headgear
3. Emergency whistle (For location signaling)
4. Jump-jets (If available in your sector)

### Test Subject Kit
1. Longer-fall boots (Emergency variant)
2. Stress-relief companion cube (Pocket-sized)
3. GLaDOS evacuation pass (Show when safe)

## Important Notes
- During Class 2 Neurotoxin events, immediately seal all access points
- Do not engage GLaDOS during Class 4 malfunctions
- Emergency cake is available at assembly point (Subject to availability)
