# Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device (ASHPD) - Operation Manual

## Device Overview
The Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device, colloquially known as the "Portal Gun," is a revolutionary tool for creating stable wormholes between two surfaces.

## Key Components
- Primary Firing Mechanism (Blue Portal)
- Secondary Firing Mechanism (Orange Portal)
- Quantum Tunneling Core
- Energy Stabilization Matrix
- Safety Lock (Activates when aimed at non-portal surfaces)

## Operating Instructions

### Step 1: Power On
- Press the green activation button on the handle
- Wait for the quantum core to stabilize (approximately 3 seconds)
- A blue indicator light confirms operational status

### Step 2: Firing Primary Portal
- Aim at any moon-dusted, white-painted surface
- Squeeze the primary trigger (blue button)
- Portal will appear with a distinctive sound signature

### Step 3: Firing Secondary Portal
- Aim at another suitable surface
- Squeeze the secondary trigger (orange button)
- Both portals will now be connected

### Step 4: Portal Travel
- Step through any portal to instantly emerge from the other
- Momentum is preserved during transit

## Important Precautions
- NEVER point the device at living subjects
- DO NOT attempt to create portals in non-permitted areas
- ALWAYS maintain minimum 2-meter distance from portal edges
- WARNING: Portal surfaces must be clean and dry for proper adhesion

## Maintenance Schedule
- Daily: Clean quantum tunneling lens
- Weekly: Calibrate energy matrix
- Monthly: Full diagnostic scan required
