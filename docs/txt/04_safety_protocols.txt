# Aperture Science Test Subject Safety Guidelines

## Introduction
Test subject safety is our top priority at Aperture Science. Following these guidelines ensures optimal testing conditions and subject well-being.

## Required Equipment
1. Aperture Science Long-Fall Boots (Prevents fall damage)
2. Advanced Knee Replacement Kit (For high-impact landings)
3. Portal Device (Issued during tests)
4. Testing Companion Cube (For emotional support)

## Pre-Test Procedures

### Step 1: Suit Up
- Wear approved testing jumpsuit
- Secure Long-Fall Boots (Velcro straps must be tight)
- Attach knee reinforcement pads
- Complete consciousness preservation waiver

### Step 2: Physical Examination
- Baseline vitals recorded
- Reflex response tested
- Jump height measured
- Portal gun grip strength evaluated

### Step 3: Briefing
- Receive test chamber objectives
- Review emergency protocols
- Sign mortality acknowledgment form

## In-Test Safety Rules

### DO
- Follow AI instructions immediately
- Use portals strategically
- Save weighted cubes for later chambers
- Press buttons marked with blue/orange indicators

### DO NOT
- Attempt to leave testing area
- Disassemble portal device
- Mock GLaDOS (She remembers everything)
- Trust promises about cake until testing is complete

## Emergency Procedures

### Portal Malfunction
- Report to GLaDOS immediately
- Avoid stepping through unstable portals
- Use emergency teleportation device (located in vestibule)

### Test Chamber Flood
- Climb to highest available surface
- Wait for emergency platform to rise
- Do not attempt to swim in neurotoxin

### High-Energy Pellet Malfunction
- Seek cover behind weighted cubes
- Alert facilities team
- Do not attempt to catch the pellet

## Post-Test Procedures
- Return portal device at exit
- Debriefing with AI system
- Receive participation certificate
- Wait for the cake (Processing time may vary)
