# Aperture Science: Company Overview

## Introduction
Aperture Science is a leading research facility specializing in advanced portal technology, artificial intelligence, and experimental physics. Founded in 1947, we have pioneered groundbreaking discoveries in quantum tunneling and sentient AI development.

## Core Technologies
1. **Portal Gun (Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device)**: Creates inter-dimensional portals for instantaneous travel
2. **GLaDOS (Genetic Lifeform and Disk Operating System)**: Advanced AI system for facility management
3. **Weighted Storage Cube**: Essential testing element with mass distribution properties
4. **Aperture Science Sentry Turret**: Automated defense system with friendly personality

## Mission Statement
To push the boundaries of science while maintaining rigorous safety protocols and test subject welfare.

## Key Facilities
- Enrichment Center (Main Testing Facility)
- GLaDOS Chamber (AI Core Housing)
- Portal Device Manufacturing Wing
- Research & Development Laboratories

## Safety Classification
All personnel must complete mandatory safety orientation before accessing any testing chambers.
